Security Methodology

BB84 Intrusion Detection

Quantum mechanics dictates that measuring an unknown quantum state irreversibly collapses its wavefunction (No-Cloning Theorem).

When Eve performs an intercept-resend attack, she guesses the wrong basis 50% of the time, causing a theoretical 25% Quantum Bit Error Rate (QBER) in the sifted key.

If the observed QBER exceeds the security threshold (11%), Alice and Bob detect the eavesdropper and immediately abort key distribution.
