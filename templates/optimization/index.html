Mathematical Principles

Max-Cut Ising Hamiltonian

Max-Cut maps to the Ising model with cost Hamiltonian:

H_C = 0.5 · ∑_{(u,v)} (I - Z_u Z_v)

The mixer Hamiltonian $H_B = \sum_i X_i$ drives quantum transitions across partitions. QAOA tunes angles $(\gamma, \beta)$ to maximize the expectation value $\langle \psi(\gamma, \beta) | H_C | \psi(\gamma, \beta) \rangle$.
